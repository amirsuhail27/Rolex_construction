About Rolex Construction

Built on precision, led by experience.

Based in Atlanta, Georgia, Rolex Construction plans and builds residential and commercial projects with the same discipline from the first site survey to the final inspection. Under the leadership of Leo, our team manages every phase in-house — engineering, permitting, contracting, and delivery — so clients work with one accountable partner instead of a chain of subcontractors.

Whether it's a custom home, a tenant build-out, or a ground-up commercial development, we treat every site the same way: measured first, built right, finished on schedule.
